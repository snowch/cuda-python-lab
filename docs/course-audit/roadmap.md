## How to judge the target

Track clarity (can the learner state the next action?), progression (can they explain why that action matters?), accessibility (can they complete it with their input and reading tools?), correctness (does it work on unseen cases?), coverage (which outcomes are taught and assessed?), reasoning (can they disprove a plausible mistake?), performance (can they measure and explain a result?) and maintainability (do regression tests preserve state and evidence?). Rate these dimensions separately at the pilot and after remediation. A starter pass and a checked completion box answer different questions.
